---
title: Update or revoke a Connection
description: Send Muse a newer Handoff, or end its access.
---

A Connection link keeps working when you approve newer Handoffs, until it expires or you revoke it.

## Send a newer Handoff

To send your assistant a newer Handoff through the same link, follow these steps:

1. In the dashboard, click **Hand off** next to the project.
2. Read the new Handoff, then click **Approve Handoff**.
3. Click **Send to Muse**.

Muse reads the newest Handoff the next time it checks. You don't need to give it a new link.

## Revoke access

- On the **Share** screen, click **Revoke share**.

The relay deletes the shared Handoff immediately, and your assistant's next request fails. To share
the project again later, publish it again and give your assistant the new link.

## When a link expires

A Connection link expires 24 hours after you publish it. To keep sharing after that, publish again
and give your assistant the new link.
